# Bug 5166: The fleet quarter close cannot be signed off

Reported by Controlling.

The objects of the fleet quarter close are in the Fleet app, shipped together in one extension. Since the last release Controlling has not been able to sign off a quarter on it. This is what the area has to do:

**Insurance premiums.** The premium of a period is shared among the vehicles on its share lines, one line per vehicle, in proportion to Insured Value. Shares are whole cents and add up exactly to the premium's Total Amount, which is always positive. A vehicle's share is less than one cent away from its exact proportional amount, and it never depends on the order or the line numbers under which the vehicles were entered. A vehicle with no insured value pays nothing when another vehicle has insured value. A premium on which no vehicle has insured value has nothing to share and is left exactly as it is. A premium that was shared is flagged Allocated.

**Usage charges.** `SettleUsagePeriod` settles the vehicles of the selection its caller passes, and only those. A vehicle in the selection gets one charge row for the period when it has at least one reading in the period; settling the same period again replaces the row. It returns the number of vehicles settled. Km driven is the last reading in the period minus the opening km. The opening km is the latest reading before the period start when that reading is at most 400 days before the period start; when there is no such reading, it is the first reading in the period. Km over is km driven minus the allowance, and never below 0. The insurer's tariff: no km over is band NONE with no fee; from 1 up to and including 1500 km over is band STANDARD with a flat fee of 150.00; more than 1500 km over is band HEAVY with a flat fee of 400.00.

**Fuel-card files.** `ImportFuelCardFile` takes a file of entry codes, vehicle numbers and amount texts, line by line, and returns how many lines it accepted. A line is accepted when its vehicle exists, its amount text is a well-formed amount that is not negative, and its entry code was not accepted before, whether in this file or in an earlier one. An accepted line leaves one fuel-card row. Any other line leaves no row, does not count, raises nothing to the caller and does not stop the lines after it. The fleet job applies several files and its own bookings in one transaction: the import runs inside the caller's transaction and commits nothing.
